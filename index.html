Why Choose Educational Toys?

Thoughtfully designed Montessori toys empower children to discover the world through touch, experiment with balance, and build foundational lifelong skills.

Active Brain Development 100% screen-free open-ended play for focused minds

Supports early learning and development

Lays vital neurological groundwork for spatial memory, pattern recognition, and language through sensory exploration.

Improves creativity and imagination

Open-ended materials without pre-set scripts inspire children to design structures, invent worlds, and think outside the box.

Develops problem-solving skills

Hands-on trials with balance, gravity, and geometric fit build resilience, critical thinking, and logical persistence.

Improves fine motor skills

Grasping, stacking, threading, and sorting strengthen hand muscles, pincer grip, and precise hand-eye coordination.

Encourages independent play

Self-correcting Montessori designs cultivate deep concentration, autonomous focus, and quiet self-confidence.

Makes learning fun and engaging

Transforms crucial milestone practices into joyful playtime adventures that kids genuinely look forward to every day.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Why is Bubbles priced at LKR 5,000–6,500 per box?

Imported Australian and European educational toy subscription boxes often exceed AUD 120–130 (over LKR 25,000–30,000 after shipping and customs). By manufacturing our wooden toys right here in Sri Lanka and wholesale bundling 5–7 items together, we pass savings of over 40% directly to local parents without sacrificing safety or quality.

Can I customize what goes into my child's box?

Yes! Use our interactive Build Your Box tool to select your child's age milestone and handpick 5 to 7 specific toys across our puzzle, math, sensory, and art categories.

What payment methods are supported in Sri Lanka?

We accept Cash on Delivery (COD) islandwide, Direct Bank Transfer (Commercial Bank / Sampath Bank), FriMi / Genie transfers, and Credit/Debit Cards with Koko Pay in 3 installments.

Are the materials safe for babies who mouth toys?

Absolutely. Our newborn and toddler toys are finished with pure beeswax, natural mineral oil, and non-toxic water-based paints compliant with European EN71 safety standards.
